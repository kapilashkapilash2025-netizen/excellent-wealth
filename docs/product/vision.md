# Product Vision

## Who this is for

- Individuals who want to understand and improve their personal finances
  without necessarily hiring a financial advisor.
- Small-business owners who need lightweight revenue/expense/cash-flow
  visibility alongside their personal finances.
- People who want to learn financial concepts through their own real (or
  demo) numbers, with every metric explained rather than presented as an
  opaque score.

## What this is not

- Not a brokerage, bank, or payments provider.
- Not a source of personalised, regulated financial, tax, investment, or
  legal advice.
- Not a guarantee of any financial outcome — all projections are labeled
  estimates based on user-supplied assumptions.

## Product pillars

1. **Explainability over black boxes.** Every score, insight, or projection
   shows its observed inputs, its calculation, and its limitations.
2. **Privacy by default.** A local demo mode lets people evaluate the product
   before any account or server-side storage is involved; exports are
   user-controlled.
3. **Correctness for money.** Financial math is decimal-safe and unit-tested
   for edge cases (zero, negative, very large, multi-currency, leap years).
4. **Accessible to everyone.** Keyboard navigation, screen-reader support,
   and reduced-motion support are first-class, not an afterthought.
